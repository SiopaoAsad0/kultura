# 🏛️ Kultura - Cultural Heritage Design System

## 🎨 **Design Philosophy**

The Kultura design system is inspired by the rich cultural heritage of the Philippines, incorporating traditional colors, patterns, and typography that reflect the beauty and wisdom of indigenous craftsmanship.

## 🌈 **Color Palette**

### Primary Colors
- **Cultural Primary**: `#8B4513` - Rich brown from traditional weaves
- **Cultural Primary Light**: `#A0522D` - Lighter brown
- **Cultural Primary Dark**: `#654321` - Darker brown

### Secondary Colors
- **Cultural Secondary**: `#D2691E` - Warm orange from annatto
- **Cultural Secondary Light**: `#F4A460` - Light sandy brown
- **Cultural Secondary Dark**: `#CD853F` - Darker sandy brown

### Accent Colors
- **Cultural Accent**: `#B8860B` - Golden yellow from turmeric
- **Cultural Accent Light**: `#DAA520` - Light gold
- **Cultural Accent Dark**: `#9A7209` - Dark gold

### Earth Tones
- **Cultural Earth**: `#8FBC8F` - Sage green from natural dyes
- **Cultural Earth Light**: `#98FB98` - Light sage
- **Cultural Earth Dark**: `#556B2F` - Dark forest green

### Neutral Colors
- **Cultural Neutral**: `#F5F5DC` - Beige from natural cotton
- **Cultural Neutral Light**: `#FFFEF7` - Cream white
- **Cultural Neutral Dark**: `#D2B48C` - Tan

## 🔤 **Typography**

### Primary Font: Playfair Display
- **Usage**: Headers, titles, and important text
- **Style**: Serif, elegant, traditional
- **Weights**: 400, 500, 600, 700

### Secondary Font: Source Sans Pro
- **Usage**: Body text, forms, and UI elements
- **Style**: Sans-serif, modern, readable
- **Weights**: 300, 400, 500, 600, 700

## 🔧 **Implementation**

### CSS Variables
All colors are defined as CSS custom properties for easy theming and maintenance.

### Component Classes
- `.cultural-font-primary` - Playfair Display typography
- `.cultural-font-secondary` - Source Sans Pro typography
- `.btn-cultural-primary` - Primary button styling
- `.card-cultural` - Cultural card styling
- `.container-cultural` - Cultural container styling

### Utility Classes
- `.cultural-section` - Section spacing
- `.cultural-text-center` - Center alignment
- `.cultural-mb-4` - Margin bottom
- `.cultural-fade-in` - Fade animation
